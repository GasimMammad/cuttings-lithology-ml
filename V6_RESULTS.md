## The question and its framing

Are the Oligocene (Hordaland Gp) sand bodies below the Top Hordaland
Unconformity (THGU) in the Tampen area sinkites? Three alternatives:

| hypothesis | what it predicts |
|---|---|
| (i) in-situ Oligocene turbidite / gravity-flow sand (Rundberg & Eidvin) | Oligocene fauna only; conformable composition |
| (ii) upward injection from a deeper parent sand (Paleocene/Eocene) | parent-sand composition; discordant bodies; no young fauna |
| (iii) sinkite: younger sand sunk into soft ooze from above | younger fauna inside the sand; composition of the overlying source; bodies hanging below THGU |

Attribution warning: Rundberg & Eidvin (2016) describe Løseth et al. (2013)
as proposing intrusion or injection, not sinking. Do not attribute
"sinkite" to Løseth without reading the primary paper. Take the definition
from the source the thesis title rests on (ask the supervisor).

## Verdict (agreed with the reviewer agent)

The released well data cannot test the age of the Oligocene sands, which is
the decisive criterion between an in-situ, injected or sinkite origin: no
sidewall cores or cores were taken in them, and ditch cuttings carry
cavings. On composition, wireline logs in 34/7-6 and cuttings XRF in three
wells show that the target sands carry little or no glauconite. That argues
against a glauconitic Utsira source, but not against sinking of sand from a
source without glauconite. The photographic green index proved not to be
specific to glauconite. An XRF index, confirmed against glauconite pellets,
found glauconite near and above the THGU, where caving and in-situ greensand
cannot be told apart. The project therefore provides **no evidence for
sinkites** and **limited compositional evidence against one specific source**.
It identifies Sr dating of hand-picked microfossils from archived cuttings as
the test that could decide the question.

## Future work that could decide it

Check G (young taxa scattered above, in and below the sand, like cavings) removes the only hint of a young component.

- Sr-isotope dating of shells and foraminifera hand-picked from the archived
  NOROG cuttings bags of the sand intervals and of the ooze above.
- New sidewall cores in any future well through these sands.
